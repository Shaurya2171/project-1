How LifeSaver Works

A donor-connect layer that sits between patients and the hospital blood bank — never a replacement for either.

For patients & families

When a hospital doesn't have your required blood type in stock, LifeSaver helps you quickly reach nearby registered donors who match your need, so you can coordinate directly while the hospital handles the medical process.

For donors

Register once, set your availability, and get notified about nearby requests that match your blood group — donate on your own terms, whenever you're able to.

What this platform is not

LifeSaver is a prototype donor directory. It does not verify medical eligibility, guarantee blood availability, or replace a hospital blood bank. Always confirm compatibility and eligibility with medical professionals.
